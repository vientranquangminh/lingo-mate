## App name (max 30)

Lingo Mate: Speak & Translate

## Short description (max 80)

Live voice translation for conversations, talks and meetings. Works offline too.

## Full description (max 4000)

Talk to anyone, follow any talk, keep up in meetings, even without internet. Every conversation becomes phrases you can reuse.

CONVERSATION
Put the phone between you. Each person taps their side and speaks; the other reads the translation the right way up and hears it aloud. Auto mode keeps one microphone open and works out who is speaking.

LISTEN
Lectures, tours, announcements: live captions in your language, large and easy to read, with the screen kept on.

MEETING
Join Zoom, Meet or Teams on your laptop and put your phone next to the speaker. Floating captions sit over your other apps, and Suggest reply offers three polite answers you can copy. After the meeting, get a summary with key terms and action items.

WORKS OFFLINE
Download a language pack and translate with no connection at all: abroad, on a plane, with poor signal. Speech recognition runs on your phone.

YOUR PHRASEBOOK
Save any line in one tap. Tag it, star it, show it in large text, play it aloud. Spaced-repetition review (Again / Hard / Good / Easy) helps you remember, with an optional daily reminder.

QUICK PHRASES
Restaurant, taxi, hotel, airport, shopping, health and more: tap a phrase and the phone says it for you.

LEARN AS YOU GO
See how a native speaker would have said your sentences, and keep a glossary of names and terms so translations use the words you prefer.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Audio is never saved; with the Offline engine it never leaves your phone. Transcripts and phrases stay on your phone. Choose the Offline engine for confidential conversations. No ads, no account needed.

Languages: Vietnamese, English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Thai, Indonesian, Spanish, French and German, with more on the way. Offline quality and features vary by language.

Please tell the people around you when you translate a conversation.
