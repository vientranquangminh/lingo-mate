Lingo Mate privacy policy

Effective 1 December 2026

Lingo Mate ("the app", "we") translates speech for face-to-face conversations, talks and online meetings. This policy explains what the app processes, where, for how long, and the choices you have. The short version: audio stays in your phone's memory and is never saved; your transcripts, phrasebook and settings stay on your phone; text is sent to Google only when you use an online engine or an online learning feature.

2. Google services

Online translation and learning features use the Gemini API through Firebase AI Logic. Google processes this text under the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms. While the app uses Gemini's free tier, Google may use the text it receives to improve its products, and people at Google may review it. Do not use the Standard engine or the online helpers for confidential conversations; switch to Offline in the engine menu instead.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not record or store audio.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. The app shows no ads.
  • The app needs no account in this version. Sign-in, if added later, will be optional and this policy will be updated first.
  • We cannot capture the audio of phone or video calls; Meeting mode only hears what your microphone hears.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: to hear speech while you use Conversation, Listen or Meeting.
  • Notifications: the persistent notification while captions run in the background, and the review reminder if you turn it on.
  • Display over other apps (optional): floating captions in Meeting mode.
  • Run at start-up: to set the review reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: online engines, language pack downloads and the services above.

5. Keeping and deleting data

  • If you signed in, delete your account in Me → your account → Delete account, or request it on the account deletion page.
  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it. Me → Privacy & data → Delete all data erases transcripts, phrases, glossaries, review cards and settings (and, if you choose, language packs). Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Text sent to Gemini is handled under Google's terms described above; we do not keep a copy on any server.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the retention period set for our Firebase project (no longer than 14 months) and are not linked to your name or contact details.

6. Telling others

Translation works by listening. Let the people around you know when you translate a conversation, talk or meeting, and follow the recording and privacy rules where you are.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Security

Connections to Google and our download server use TLS. Data on your phone sits in the app's private storage, protected by Android.

9. Changes

We will update this page, and its effective date, before any change in what the app collects. Important changes will also be shown in the app.
